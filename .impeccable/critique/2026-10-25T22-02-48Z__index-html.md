#### Overall Impression
A stunning, highly disciplined architectural diorama with rare domain authenticity and calm aesthetic focus. The primary opportunities lie in streamlining touch navigation in the Architecture Palette, eliminating legacy AI-slop side-tab borders and neon glows, and deduplicating navigation entry points.

#### What's Working
1. **Model-First Supremacy**: The 3D diorama canvas claims the entire viewport (`100dvh`), with floating chrome occupying under 6% of screen area at rest.
2. **Honesty Over Automation**: Clear separation between unmeasured provisional rooms (Amber tags) and tape-verified geometry (Sage badges), protecting movers from costly furniture mistakes.
3. **Completely Self-Contained Offline Privacy**: Zero external CDN calls, trackers, or cloud storage; private uncompressed ZIP backup exports keep personal spaces secure.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Architecture Palette Touch Choice Overload**:
  - *Why it matters*: The Architecture Palette displays 18 tool buttons in a dense 5-column icon-only grid (`font-size: 0!important`). On mobile touchscreens without hover tooltips, users must blind-tap abstract geometric symbols.
  - *Fix*: Categorize the palette into 3-4 progressive disclosure tabs (e.g., Walls & Shapes, Openings, Zones, Finishes) with concise text labels.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Slop Side-Tab Borders & Neon Glows**:
  - *Why it matters*: Thick colored left borders on `.tip`, `.survey-reason`, `.viewport-hud`, and neon glow on `.rail-button.active::before` breach `DESIGN.md`'s 1px structural framing doctrine and inject generic AI tropes into a handcrafted diorama.
  - *Fix*: Replace left borders with 1px perimeter borders (`1px solid var(--border-line)`) and replace the zero-offset glow with a subtle directional ambient shadow.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish`
- **[P2] Redundant Dual IA Entry Points**:
  - *Why it matters*: "Design Lab" and "Survey Studio" are launched from both the top-left Project header and the bottom Tools drawer, splitting user mental models.
  - *Fix*: Anchor global studio suites strictly in the Project header, reserving the bottom dock for active diorama manipulation tools.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable distill`
- **[P2] Tape Measure Buried in Secondary Drawer**:
  - *Why it matters*: The core value proposition is verifying tape measurements, but the Tape tool is hidden inside the Tools drawer while Build and Add take primary dock space.
  - *Fix*: Surface the Tape Measure affordance directly in the Context Selection Chip when two objects/walls are selected, or provide a quick toggle in the dock.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable shape tape-workflow`
- **[P3] Layout Reflow on Survey Progress Bar**:
  - *Why it matters*: `#surveyOverallBar` animates `width .2s` triggering browser reflows during survey progress updates.
  - *Fix*: Animate via `transform: scaleX(...)` with `transform-origin: left`.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable optimize`
